About IIEE

A practical institution with a national purpose.

IIEE exists to make engineering education useful where theory meets industry.

01

History

Established in 1989 as a joint project of the Government of Pakistan and the Government of Switzerland, IIEE was created under the Ministry of Science & Technology and PCSIR to close the gap between theoretical engineering education and practical industrial training.

02

Mission

To produce Industrial Electronics Engineering graduates contributing towards sustainable industrial development for a progressive and prosperous society.

Our students learn to think in systems, test with discipline, and build for the world beyond the classroom.
